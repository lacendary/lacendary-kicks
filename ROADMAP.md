# Guiding Principles

## Document, Don't Review

Lacendary Kicks is not built around opinions.

It is built around documentation.

---

## Quality Over Quantity

Every page should feel handcrafted.

---

## Cinematic First

Photography.

Video.

Music.

Interaction.

Story.

---

## Evergreen Content

Focus on creating content that remains valuable years after publication.

---

## Experience First

Every feature should make exploring sneakers more enjoyable.

If it doesn't improve the experience, it doesn't belong.
